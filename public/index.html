ANSWERS
Direct Answers

Clear answers. No fluff.

How does Hoversight differ from a standard software agency?

Agencies work on project-based handoffs: they build, invoice, hand over a login, and leave. Hoversight operates as an embedded tech department — staying on to hover over your systems, optimize performance, and continuously engineer your roadmap.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You own 100% of the code, data, and intellectual property. We build directly on your cloud infrastructure or hand over complete code repositories with clean documentation.

What if we already have software that is broken or unmaintained?

We specialize in legacy codebase takeover. We audit your existing stack, stabilize critical operations, build evaluation suites, and resume active feature development under our continuous guardianship.

How quickly can Hoversight move in and start shipping?

Our discovery phase begins immediately. We shadow your operational team during Week 1 and deliver a working, live proof-of-concept on real data within 14 to 21 days.
