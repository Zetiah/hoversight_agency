Cookie Policy

Effective 13 August 2026 · Last updated 13 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Hoversight uses cookies and similar technologies on hoversight.agency.

In short: we keep it minimal. This site uses only the cookies strictly necessary to serve the page securely. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers, so there's no consent banner to click through.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to site operators.

2. How we use cookies

We currently use only strictly necessary cookies — those required for the site and our hosting platform to load and function securely. These do not track you across websites and are not used for advertising or profiling.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or social-media tracking cookies on this site.

3. Third-party services

If you follow a link to a third-party service (for example, our scheduling provider), that service may set its own cookies governed by its own policies. We encourage you to review the policies of any third-party sites you visit.

4. Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Because we only use strictly necessary cookies, blocking them may prevent parts of the site from working correctly, but you will not lose any tracking-based features (there are none).

5. Changes to this policy

If we introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies in the future, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent before setting them.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@hoversight.agency.
