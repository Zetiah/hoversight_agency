Privacy Policy

Effective 13 August 2026 · Last updated 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hoversight ("Hoversight", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit hoversight.agency or contact us. We keep the data we collect to a minimum and never sell it.

Have a question about your data, or want a copy or deletion of it? Email info@hoversight.agency and we'll take care of it.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you use the contact form or email us, we collect the details you choose to provide — typically your name, email address, and the message you send. If you book a call, our scheduling provider collects the information needed to schedule it.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting platform records basic technical data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) in server logs for security and reliability. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and communicate with you;
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services and this website;
  • To keep the site and our systems secure and prevent abuse;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (when you contact us), our legitimate interests in operating and securing our business, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the site and business (for example, hosting and scheduling providers), under agreements that require them to protect it;
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data retention

We keep enquiry and contact information only as long as needed to respond to you and for our legitimate business and legal purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email info@hoversight.agency. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International transfers

Hoversight operates from the Philippines and works with clients in Canada and globally. Your information may be processed in countries other than your own; where required, we take steps to ensure it remains protected.

9. Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
